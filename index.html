About Us

Born from a divine calling

Divine Melodies was born from a divine calling on August 22, 2026, when a group of passionate Christian musicians from the Dallas-Fort Worth area came together with a shared vision: to use the gift of music for evangelization and to sing for the glory of God.

Our ministry is built upon the solid foundation of Scripture, particularly Colossians 3:16. This verse encapsulates everything we stand for—allowing Christ's message to flow through our music, teaching and encouraging others, and singing with grateful hearts.

We are called to spread the Gospel through the power of music, touching hearts and souls across the DFW area and beyond. Our songs are not just melodies—they are messages of hope, faith, and God's eternal love.

Our Mission

Christ's love, brought to life through music

Through worship services, evangelization events, and community outreach, we bring Christ's love to life through music. We share the Gospel at local events and serve our community with hope and healing.

Our Foundation

Rooted in God's Word

Our foundation rests firmly on God's Word, with Colossians 3:16 as our guiding light. This scripture calls us to let Christ's message dwell richly within us, teaching and encouraging one another through psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs.

Music becomes our vessel for ministry—a divine tool to uplift spirits, strengthen faith, and draw hearts closer to the Lord. Each melody carries purpose, each harmony reflects His glory.
